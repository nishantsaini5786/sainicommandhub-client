Wireshark is an interactive network protocol analyzer. Use it to capture traffic, inspect packet fields, filter noisy traces, follow protocol streams and investigate PCAP/PCAPNG files in an authorized lab or network.

Core Concept

Wireshark gives you three complementary views of traffic: a packet summary, decoded protocol details, and raw packet bytes. Display filters narrow what you see without deleting packets from the capture. Capture filters are applied while capturing and use libpcap syntax, which is different from Wireshark's display-filter language.

Important: -f is a capture filter, while -Y is a display filter. Capture filters are generally more efficient during live capture; display filters are richer and are ideal for investigating an existing capture.

PCAP Investigation Workflow

1. Open the capture and check the time range and interface metadata.

2. Start with broad filters such as dns, tcp, http, or tls.

3. Identify important IPs, ports, DNS names and TCP conversations.

4. Inspect TCP flags, retransmissions, resets and zero-window events when diagnosing connectivity.

5. Use Follow Stream when you need to understand a particular conversation.

6. Correlate packet timestamps with application or host logs instead of interpreting a single packet in isolation.

Wireshark vs TShark

Wireshark: graphical packet inspection, protocol trees, coloring rules, stream following, statistics and interactive filtering.

TShark: terminal-oriented packet capture and analysis. It can read/write PCAPNG, apply capture/display filters and export selected fields, making it useful for scripts and repeatable analysis.

Troubleshooting

No interfaces: check permissions, libpcap support and whether the expected interface is up.

Packets missing: verify the selected interface, capture filter, capture buffer and traffic volume.

Filter turns red: check field names and syntax; Wireshark's filter toolbar validates expressions while you type.

Encrypted traffic: TLS/SSH encryption limits application-content visibility unless appropriate session keys or other authorized decryption material is available.

⚠
AUTHORIZED USE ONLY

Capture and analyze traffic only on systems and networks you own or have explicit permission to monitor. PCAP files can contain credentials, cookies, personal data and other sensitive information; store and share them accordingly.
